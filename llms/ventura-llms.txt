# Ventura

> Ventura (operated by Gradient Ascent Labs, Inc., YC W26, San Francisco) builds an AI
> workforce for industrial distributors and manufacturers, starting with quoting and
> order entry. It reads inbound emails, PDFs, images and phone calls, matches products
> against the customer's catalog, drafts a quote or order for a human rep to review, and
> pushes the approved result into the customer's ERP. As of 2026-08-14 Ventura publishes
> no public API, developer portal, documentation, SDK or machine-readable specification;
> the product is delivered as a hosted application with ERP/CRM integrations arranged
> during onboarding.
